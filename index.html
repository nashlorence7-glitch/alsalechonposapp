How do I get the Page ID and token?
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com, open My Apps, create an app (Business type) and add the Messenger product.
  2. Open the Graph API Explorer, pick your app, then Get Page Access Token. Choose your page and allow pages_show_list, pages_messaging, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_metadata.
  3. Run GET me/accounts. It lists your page with its id (the Page ID) and access_token.
  4. Paste both above and tap Connect.
Tokens made in the Explorer expire in about an hour. For one that lasts, first extend your user token in Facebook's Access Token Debugger, then run me/accounts. Until Meta approves your app (Advanced Access for pages_messaging), you will only see chats with people who have a role on your app. The token is saved only on this device; Disconnect removes it.
